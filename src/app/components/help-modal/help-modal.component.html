How Adattamento works

1. Upload a photo of yourself

Front-facing, showing your upper or full body, with a plain background works best. Relax your arms at your sides.

2. Add a clothing item

Use a product photo from any shopping site — even one where the item is worn by a model. Clear, well-lit images give the best results.

3. Run the try-on

We'll generate an image of you wearing the item. Use "Try Another" to keep your photo and try a different outfit.

Each try-on uses one generation from your monthly limit.
